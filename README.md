# E-Commerce Refund Process

## 📌 Project Overview

An end-to-end Business Analyst portfolio project focused on designing and documenting a user refund process for an e-commerce application.

The project demonstrates how a Business Analyst can analyze a business problem, define requirements, create user stories, map the process, identify stakeholder responsibilities, and evaluate the expected business value of a proposed feature.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Customers may experience uncertainty when requesting refunds because the refund process and status are not sufficiently transparent or trackable.

This can result in repeated customer enquiries and additional workload for the customer-care team.

The project targets a reduction in refund-related customer complaints from approximately **8 complaints per week to 4 complaints per week**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective is to design a clearer and more trackable refund process that:

- Improves refund-process transparency
- Helps customers understand refund status
- Reduces repeated refund-related enquiries
- Defines clear responsibilities across stakeholders
- Provides a structured process from return initiation to refund completion

## 📂 Project Deliverables

### 1. Business Requirements Document

The BRD defines the proposed refund feature, including:

- Business problem
- Objective
- Scope
- Business requirements
- Stakeholders
- Constraints
- Cost-benefit considerations

📁 `BRD/`

---

### 2. User Stories

User stories were created to represent the customer's needs throughout the refund journey.

The documented scenarios include:

- Returning a product
- Requesting a refund
- Providing a refund reason
- Receiving refund decision notifications
- Handing over the product for pickup

Acceptance criteria and edge cases are included for the user stories.

📁 `User-Stories/`

---

### 3. BPMN Process Flow

The BPMN process maps the refund journey from the customer's return/refund request through the relevant process steps and completion.

The process flow helps stakeholders understand:

- Activities
- Process sequence
- Roles
- Decisions
- Handoffs
- Exceptions

📁 `BPMN/`

---

A RACI matrix was created to clarify stakeholder responsibilities for activities involved in developing and launching the refund feature.

Stakeholder roles include:

- Product Manager
- Business Analyst
- Developer
- QA
- Marketing
- Customer Support

📁 `RACI/`

## 📋 Key Business Requirements

The project identifies requirements related to:

- Notifying the customer about refund acceptance or rejection

- Processing the refund

The requirements are prioritized based on business importance.

## 🔄 Refund Process

The high-level customer journey is:

**Delivered Order**

↓

**Customer Initiates Return**

↓

**Return Eligibility Check**

↓

**Return Request Created**

↓

**Customer Requests Refund**

↓

**Refund Reason Provided**

↓

**Refund Reason Validated**

↓

**Refund Accepted / Rejected**

↓

**Customer Notification**

↓

**Product Pickup / Handover**

↓

**Refund Process Completion**

---

## ⚠️ Edge Cases Considered

The project considers scenarios such as:

- Return period has expired
- Duplicate refund request
- Customer does not provide a refund reason
- Notification failure
- Delivery person does not arrive for product pickup

These cases are documented to ensure that the process considers situations outside the normal flow.
