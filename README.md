Overview
End-to-end sales data analysis project covering SQL querying, statistical analysis, and dashboard reporting on a multi-country, multi-segment sales dataset (2013–2014).
Problem
Leadership lacks a clear view of where sales and profit are actually concentrated — across products, segments, countries, and months — making it hard to guide discounting and regional strategy decisions.
Objective
Answer 5 core business questions using SQL, identify key sales/profit patterns through statistical analysis, and present findings in an interactive Power BI dashboard for stakeholder use.
What's Included
File	What it shows
SQL_Questions_For_Product_Dataset.docx	5 business questions answered in SQL, each with query + verified output
Stats_Summary.xlsx	Mean, median, mode, and standard deviation of sales, with a 4-line business interpretation
Product_Report.pbix	Power BI dashboard: Total Sales, Sales by Product/Country, Profit by Segment/Country, Top 5 Products, with a Month slicer
Summary_of_the_Sales_Report.docx	1-paragraph executive summary of key findings
Key Findings
•	Average sale value: ₹169,609 | Median sale value: ₹35,540 — the large gap indicates a small number of big deals skew the average upward; the median is the more representative "typical" sale.
•	Top product by sales: Paseo (₹33.0M)
•	Top segment (sales & profit): Government (₹52.5M sales / ₹11.4M profit) — also the segment with the lowest discounting.
•	Top country (sales & profit): USA (₹25.0M sales / ₹3.0M profit)
•	Top month by sales: October (₹21.7M)
